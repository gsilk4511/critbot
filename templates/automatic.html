Create an automatic reward

Set a goal, choose the reward, and let CritBot award it when someone qualifies.

What should members accomplish?

1 message per minute can count; 100 doesn't mean 100 messages in one minute.

The description is generated from the goal. You can customize artwork and rewards later under Achievements.

This achievement will be automatically awarded when the goal is reached. Changing its current milestone target is supported; switching its activity type is not.

Rewards are awarded once per member. Existing progress is honored on their next qualifying activity.
